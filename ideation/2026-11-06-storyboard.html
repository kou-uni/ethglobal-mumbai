Why the refusal is shown on the Ledger

An out-of-scope request is stopped by the Broker, so it never reaches the Ledger. Even so, the fact of the refusal is shown on the Ledger as a notification (no signature is asked for; just the ✕ and the reason).

A refusal that never reaches your hand is an invisible refusal.

A refusal you can't see isn't a refusal.
