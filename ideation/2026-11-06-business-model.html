01The business model

We are paid for deciding — the same for yes as for no. Four things are cut off by design, and each one, sold, would end the sentence the product is made of.

Why STOPPED is not billed. It means our own sealed model was unreachable, so no verdict exists. Billing it would mean we earn when we are down — and a company that earns while failing stops noticing that it failed.

02Lean canvas

The full text, with the gaps it had to decide and the contradiction register, is in LEAN-CANVAS.md. This is the one-page view.

The early adopter is P2, the maker — not P1. P1 is the sympathetic persona and the evidence that P1 pays a subscription is zero. P2 already has a recurring budget line for being trusted. Sell to the side that already pays for trust.

The cash point that made us change A's shape. $108 billed once arrives at the start of the year it covers. The same amount at $9/month arrives twelve times, after the cost of serving it. Annual billing is not a pricing trick here; it is the working capital.

What this table does not contain. No funding round, no salary for the two of us in year 1, no enterprise sales team. If any of the three were needed, the shape of row 3 changes before the margin does.
