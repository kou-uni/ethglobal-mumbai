Who puts what in, and what comes out

Not one yen moves inside this product. Both the deposit (staking tokens) and the insurance (paying a premium) were dropped in the early hours of 11/6. What is staked now is “your own key itself”. On that basis, the inputs, outputs, stakes and returns of everyone involved are on one page.

2026-11-06kouAssumes: Decision 1 (drop the deposit), Decision 9 (flames are per version)

Executive summary

  1. A person puts in only three things. One signature on the 4 lines of the scope / one press per heavy request / one vouch through World. Nothing else goes in (no key, no money, no personal data).
  2. Four things come out. The judgement of whether it passed or was stopped / a single-use permit / one flame / a trail that cannot be erased. Every output is a fact, and not one of them contains a promise.
  3. The deposit and the insurance were dropped (Decision 1). A deposit inverts into “the more money you hold, the more you are trusted”, and a premium may run into insurance regulation. What is staked instead is your own key—the guarantor stopped being someone who vouches for another and became someone in the same boat.
  4. Money moves along exactly one path. User → TOMOSHIBI, as a monthly fee and metered usage. There are no transfers between users, and no percentage of payment volume.

03What happened to “staking tokens” and “taking out insurance”

Both were dropped (early hours of 11/6, Decision 1). The reason was not time; it was that putting them in inverts the product's claim.

Table 2 The old design → the current design
What we once intendedHow it breaksWhat stands there instead now
The deposit (staking tokens)
A guarantor deposits a small sum, which goes to the victim on a violation		 It inverts into the more money you hold, the more you are trusted. The flame, which should be the fact of who is using it, becomes a ranking you can buy We changed what is staked to the key itself. Only someone using that version with their own key can light a flame. If it goes wrong, they are harmed too
Insurance (the user pays a premium; if nothing goes wrong, the guarantor gets a top-up) It may run into insurance regulation [it cannot go into the product while that stays unverified]. On top of that, it would mean selling the false reassurance that paying money makes you safe What we sell is a record, not reassurance. The fact of having stopped something, and the fact of having been refused, remain. We do not indemnify losses (and we do not claim we can)
Tips (“thank you” payments) and secondary matching A money flow appears between users, and the flame becomes a source of income. The moment a motive to buy exists, the flame loses its meaning We do not build it. The flame is not for sale (the same column as “What we will not do” in the business model)
Three tiers of vouch strength, and zero-knowledge Cannot be built safely in 36 hours. Half-done anonymisation creates the misunderstanding that nothing is leaking Cut down to two tiers, linked by the pseudonym (World's sub) alone. We write honestly that within the same app it is visible that it is the same person

If we ever do them. The deposit would be a separate axis, never mixed into the weight of a flame (“how much this person has staked” is never shown next to the flame); the insurance would be handing the trail to a third-party insurer. Neither gets built this time, and neither is mentioned in the video. If asked, we answer straight from this table.

One old diagram remains. The architecture diagram in ideation/2026-11-06-final-image.md still draws VouchRegistry (vouching, deposit). That is a version from before Decision 1. We did not swap the diagram out; we added a mark so that it reads as “up to here, there was a deposit”.
