    Trail (append only)

      This screen is a prototype. minta will finish the design. All that is settled here is what appears, in what order, and at what size.

      The 0G Private Computer is not decoration. Deciding weight means reading the body of the request, and we do not want that body shown to us. So it is read inside the seal (TEE). Every verdict returns a request id, and it appears in the table above.

      When it cannot be reached, no verdict is invented. No default, no guess on the spot (STOPPED). A false verdict is as bad as letting the call through.
