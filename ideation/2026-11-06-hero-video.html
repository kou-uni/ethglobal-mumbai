Hero video · rough sketch

With it on the left. Without it on the right. 68 seconds.

The split never breaks. Every beat is a thing that happens to the viewer, not a step in our flow — nobody watching a hero video wants to learn a procedure. This is the loop at the top of the page; the 2–4 minute judged video is separate and sits in ideation/2026-11-06-storyboard.html.

01Why this shape

A hero video has about four seconds to be understood. A split screen is the only format where the viewer does the comparing and we do not have to explain.

  • Value, not procedure. We never show how a permit is constructed. We show what is different on the morning after
  • The right side is not a strawman. It is what everyone does today, and it works — right up until beat 3
  • Nothing is mocked. Every verdict on the left is a real response from the running Broker. If a frame cannot be produced for real, the frame is cut, not faked
  • It reads with the sound off. Judges scroll. The voice-over is a bonus, not the carrier

02The cut list

Eight beats. The first three carry it; if time runs short, cut beats 5 and 7, in that order.

00:00 – 00:06 1. The same agent, the same job

Voice: “Two people. Same agent. Same job.”
Must be real: Both sides run the same request body. Record them in one session so the timestamps match.

00:06 – 00:14 2. What you handed over

Voice: “On the left you hand over one use. On the right you hand over the key.”
Must be real: Left: the real four lines on the device screen. Right: a real key string, blurred after the first six characters.

00:14 – 00:24 3. The agent asks for something else

Voice: “Now the agent asks for something you never approved.”
Must be real: A real DENIED from the Broker, with the destination visible. The right side is a real 200.

00:24 – 00:34 4. Money is about to move, and nobody has pressed

Voice: “Irreversible. On the left it waits for a person. On the right it is already done.”
Must be real: The press has to be a real press on the device. No cut between the screen and the finger.

00:34 – 00:42 5. Our own checker goes down

Voice: “When we cannot decide, we stop and change nothing. We do not guess, and we do not charge.”
Must be real: Point the Broker at an unreachable endpoint. Do not fake the error.

00:42 – 00:52 6. The morning after

Voice: “Something went wrong. On the right, every line says failed. On the left, each stop has a name and an owner.”
Must be real: Show the real trail and the real transaction hash. It has to be clickable on the explorer.

00:52 – 01:00 7. A new version ships

Voice: “A review score never expires. A flame goes out.”
Must be real: The stars screen before and after a version bump. One take, no cut.

01:00 – 01:08 8. The line

Voice: “Your agent is holding a spare key that works until you cancel it. It does not have to.”
Must be real: Last frame holds the repo URL for three full seconds.

04What this video must not do

  • No architecture diagram. Not one box. It is in the README for the reader who wants it
  • No logo animation. Four seconds of brand is four seconds of the budget
  • No claim we cannot click. If a number appears on screen it appears in the README too
  • Do not say “secure”. Show a refusal instead
