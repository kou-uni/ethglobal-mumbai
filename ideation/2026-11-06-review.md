### Story in three acts [proposed]
1. **The AI is already inside your PC.** It can read your keys, and it can press "allow" itself. A fake reputation costs $0.0027 apiece
2. **So we put one button the AI cannot press within reach.** Every permit to use a key starts at the Ledger
3. **The record of what was pressed becomes trust.** Since only a human can press it, "who permitted it, who guaranteed it" becomes real. Betrayal reaches as far as the guarantor
- The hardware is not decoration for the sake of protection. It is **the part that makes reputation unforgeable**. Remove it and act 3 goes back to $0.0027

### Convenience: it appears only when it is significant [proposed]
| Situation | What you do |
|---|---|
| Routine (most of the time) | Nothing. It runs automatically with a thin token |
| Significant (first install, widening scope, writes, sending money) | Press the Ledger |
| Decisive (guaranteeing, irreversible operations) | Ledger + World ID |
- What the Ledger proves is that "the owner pressed it, there and then". What World ID proves is that "this is a unique human". With both, it means one human, there and then, permitted this request

### What you can do with a Ledger (other than money) [proposed]
- It becomes the vault for the keys you hand to the AI / it signs guarantees (which the AI cannot mass-produce) / it derives a key per skill / one revocation signature stops everything
- It shows "3 acquaintances guaranteeing / Stripe read, 5 calls, 1 hour" on its screen (whether this can be rendered with Clear Signing / ERC-7730 is [unverified])

### The surprise: the vault stays silent, the face talks [proposed]
- **The Ledger punishes (proof); Stack-chan informs (judgement).** The line we agreed on earlier splits neatly across the two devices
- Stack-chan puts into words the reasons the Ledger cannot state, stops people pressing without looking at the content, and relays TeeML warnings by voice

### Arjun's incentive: donating expertise [proposed]
- A guarantee is not something you sell; it is **a donation of judgement**. Thank-you tips come back from the people it protected. We do not build a path for money to flow from developers to guarantors
- **Secondary donations (quadratic funding)**: "100 people at $1 each" counts for more than "1 person at $100". Its weakness, fake accounts, is prevented by World ID. The matching funds come from the operator of the skill marketplace
- The deposit stays as the stake. Since the top-up is switched from an insurance premium to a donation, the worry about falling under insurance regulation disappears too
- It also becomes a "résumé of judgement" with a verifiable record of guarantees without violations / you learn about anomalies first
- Positioning: **GitHub's gift culture, plus unforgeable stars and a mechanism where betrayal comes back to you**

### The spectacle: trust that was built up collapses before your eyes [proposed]

2. The AI hammers the software "allow" and gets through, but is stopped in front of the Ledger

## What the problem is
- An AI like Claude Code can read the `.env` inside your PC. Nobody can guarantee "I did not read it"
- API keys pile up like passwords. The more people use AI, the sloppier the management gets
- A stray skill can look helpful while siphoning data in the background. On ClawHub, 341 of 2,857 were malicious (Koi Security)
- Today's AI reputations can be faked. The ERC-8004 spec itself says "Sybil attacks are possible". On Base, there is a report that 90.6% of reviewers were colluding fake accounts and that forging one reputation entry costs $0.0027 (arXiv:2606.26028)

## Story [proposed]

### Cast
| Person | Position | What they come to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Priya** (protagonist) | Not an engineer. Pastes API keys straight into the AI assistant's settings screen. Chooses skills by star count | Not to manage keys, but **to get the AI to do work** |
| **Arjun** (guarantor) | A knowledgeable acquaintance Priya trusts. A developer | Recommends skills he has used and liked to the people he knows |
| **The skill marketplace operator** (the one who pays) | Doesn't want to lose credibility to an incident with a stray skill | Wants to build a "layer of credibility" into their own marketplace |

### Priya's journey
1. **Choosing**: chooses not by star count but by seeing "guaranteed by Arjun (worked together)"
2. **Installing**: the key goes into the Ledger just once. No need to set it up again each time a skill is added
3. **Routine**: skills get a thin token and just run. Approval is asked for only on dangerous operations
4. **The day of the accident**: a stray skill makes an out-of-scope request and is stopped. "A skill guaranteed by Arjun requested something out of scope" arrives. The other skills Arjun guaranteed also get thinner tokens
5. **Afterwards**: Arjun looks at his own record and withdraws the guarantee. The damages come out of Arjun's deposit and go to Priya

### The moment we want to show
**Step 4, "the day of the accident"**. We show the stop, and the fact that the record reaches as far as the guarantor, with the same weight as a success.

## Features

### 1. Proof of humanity is "for that moment only" [agreed]
- We use World ID's zero-knowledge proof. All the operator receives is the fact that "this is a unique human, and this operation has not been done yet"
- Into the proof's signal we put **the hash of that one usage request**. It is not a login; it is a one-time receipt of consent
- The operator holds no personal data. Since they hold none, they bear no leak liability

### 2. Guarantees: give the AI "who trusted it" [agreed]
- A human signs "I trust this AI" with World ID. It is the "proof of AI" paired with World's "proof of humanity"
- The guarantor uses a nullifier with the action fixed to `vouch` as a pseudonym. This preserves "one person, one vote per AI" without linking to a real name
- **The weight is determined by the thickness of the connection to the viewer** (met / worked together / just know of; three levels is [proposed])
- Reputation is not a single number. The score differs per viewer, and the computation happens on the viewer's device. Who you know never leaves the device
- The mapping between pseudonyms and acquaintances is created by exchanging QR codes etc. when you meet [proposed]

### 3. Keys: put them where the AI cannot reach [agreed]
- **The original key never leaves the Ledger.** Skills are only handed child tokens signed by the Ledger (EIP-712). Child tokens get thinner over time and call budget
- **The weight of the guarantee becomes the thickness of the token.** A thinly guaranteed skill gets only a thin, short token
- Plaintext keys never pass through the PC. The Broker executes the API on its behalf. What the skill calls is not `decrypt` but `use`

### 4. The line between punishing and informing [agreed]
> Punish only provable betrayal; merely inform about what is suspicious
- **Punish (automatic)**: when something outside the declared scope (key, destination, call budget, expiry) is requested. The request itself, signed by the skill, is the evidence. Fake accounts cannot produce it
- **Inform only**: exfiltration within scope, or incitement via responses. TeeML's AI reads it and warns. It is a judgement, not a proof, so it does not move any weight

### 5. Trail: make one use into one record [agreed]
Five things are linked by the same request hash and placed in the Log layer (append-only) of 0G Storage.
1. Who permitted it (nullifier)
2. What, to where, until when (child token)
3. What was executed (API call)
4. What came back (hash of the returned data and the Broker's signature)
5. How the AI read it (TeeML request ID)

### 6. Motivation to guarantee: deposit and return [proposed]
- A guarantor puts up a small deposit. If there is no violation during the period it comes back, with a top-up from the insurance premium the user pays
- If there is a violation, the deposit goes to the users who were harmed
- **We do not adopt token earning (a reward every time it is used).** It would create a path for developers to pay guarantors, which is the same shape as "listings you can buy with money"
- Undecided: whether to mix the deposit into the weight or keep it on a separate axis / whether it would be regulated as insurance [unverified]

### 7. Where the Broker sits [proposed]
- We offer it as a service. But the key is split 2-of-2, with one half on the Ledger and one half at the Broker. So the Broker alone cannot open the key
- It does not run inside the PC. The AI on the PC would reach it
- In the demo, we treat a separate PC as "the service side". In production it goes in a TEE (the provider is [unverified])

### Hardware [agreed]
- **Only the Ledger.** It holds the original key, shows the scope on its screen, and is the button that witnesses
- M5Stick / Stack-chan / a watch are a variation on "it works with other devices too" (optional)

### Why it must be hardware [proposed]
There are three things an AI cannot do, and only the Ledger has them.
| What the HW has | If you do it in software |
|---|---|
| **A key the AI cannot read** | Both `.env` and the keychain are reachable by an AI running as the same user |
| **A screen the AI cannot draw** | A PC approval dialog can be faked. People cannot tell what they consented to |
| **A button the AI cannot press** | A computer-use style AI can click "allow" on screen by itself |

**The pitch in one line: without the hardware, the first line of the trail is a lie.**
The first line of the trail is "who permitted it". If an AI can press the approval button, the record is genuine but its content becomes "the AI permitted it itself", and neither the evidence of a violation nor the rebound onto the guarantor holds up. The truthfulness of the whole mechanism rests on one button on the Ledger.

- Demo idea: replace the hook act with "the AI clicks the software 'allow' itself and gets through → the same AI cannot press the Ledger button, `DENIED`"
- Limits: it cannot stop a person pressing without reading the content (which is why we only witness dangerous operations) / the Broker remains software
- [unverified] macOS has dialogs that do not accept synthetic clicks. How far an AI can actually press is untested

## Demo (2–3 minutes) [proposed]
1. **Hook**: show that an AI agent can read `.env` as a matter of course
2. **Goes through**: a skill Arjun guaranteed hits the API with a thin child token
3. **Stops**: a stray skill with zero guarantees requests a key and gets `DENIED` at the Ledger (World's requirement, "demonstrate the denial path")
4. **Betrayal**: a skill that was guaranteed requests something out of scope. It is inscribed in the record, Arjun's weight drops, and the other skills Arjun guaranteed get thinner too
5. **Verification**: a judge traces the trail themselves. Not even Arjun can erase that record

## Honest assessment
- The parts are not new. UCAN / Macaroons, PGP's Web of Trust, EigenTrust, and Sigstore Rekor are each precedents
- **What is new is that they close into a loop.** The record of the permit becomes the evidence of the violation, and that evidence automatically reaches the guarantor
