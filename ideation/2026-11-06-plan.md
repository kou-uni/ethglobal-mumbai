## 1. Execution policy (first draft)

### Policy 1: build the Ledger side as a custom app. But secure the floor first

**Changed 2026-11-06 (#11, minta).** Until then it was "don't aim for a custom app".
The reason was "it needs an embedded SDK, a build environment and device management mode, and in 36 hours it very likely won't be ready".
**That premise is gone: a custom app is already running on real hardware on the Flex.** The heaviest hurdle is already cleared.

→ **Aim for the custom app. Respect D3's cap (4 hours total).**
→ **Clear gate 0 (the standard app) first, so even if we fail, the floor always remains.**

| Gate | Contents | Estimate | If it doesn't pass |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0** | Whether the scope renders in the standard app (#5). **Secure the floor first** | 30 min | Alternative a (a single-line string) |
| 1 | Display the 4 scope lines in the custom app and get a signature back from a long press. **And the Broker's `ecrecover` produces the same address** | 1.5 hours | Film at level 3 (the standard app) |
| 2 | The ★ stamp, and the black-and-white-inverted `DENIED` (a notification that requires no signature) | 1 hour | Stop partway through level 2 |
| 3 | The constellation | If there is room | Don't do it |

**The three things that cannot be dropped stay the same**: (1) the scope is readable (2) it doesn't go through unless you press (3) the denial appears in your hand.

**The signature hand-off is fixed in `spec/permit-signature.md`.**
One and the same JSON, for both the standard app and the custom app. If this disagrees,
**the app works but the Broker DENIEDs everything**, which is why gate 1's pass criterion includes
"the Broker's `ecrecover` produces the same address".

**Keep the way of filming at level 3 as it is in script v4.** Show the denial as "the Ledger never asks at all",
with the subtitle "The Ledger **does not even show this request to a human**".
**Design-wise this is actually more accurate** (out-of-scope is stopped at the Broker, so it never reaches the real device).
